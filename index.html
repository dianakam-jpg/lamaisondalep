Who We Are

About La Maison d'Alep

La Maison d'Alep is a traditional Syrian soap house operating in Türkiye, following the original Aleppo soap craftsmanship passed down for over 1,200 years.

We produce a wide range of natural soaps based on olive oil and selected vegetable oils — from cooking, cutting, and stamping to curing and showroom display — all carried out in our own facility under direct supervision.

Our range spans Aleppo Laurel Soap, natural liquid soap, glycerin soap, scented pressed soap, and hair care. We supply wholesalers, importers, and distributors worldwide, including natural skincare importers, retail chains, private label brands, specialized retailers, hotels, and spa resorts — with consistent quality and reliable production capacity for export.

The Craft

From the cauldron to the cure — inside our workshop.

Every batch follows the same sequence our craftsmen have used for generations, photographed on our own production floor.

01

Pour

The cooked paste is spread across the floor in a single even layer.

02

Score

While still soft, the surface is scored into a precise grid.

03

Mark the Lines

Straight guide lines are drawn by hand before the final cut.

04

Cut & Separate

Bars are cut and arranged into rows to begin drying.

05

Stack

Bars are stacked in ventilated rows across the warehouse floor.

06

Cure

Bars cure for a minimum of nine months, mellowing to their signature amber tone.
